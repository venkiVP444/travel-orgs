# TravelOrgOS - 10-Minute Sales Demonstration Script

This script walks a sales engineer through presenting the value proposition of **TravelOrgOS** to a travel agency client.

---

## 1. Intro Narrative (60 Seconds)
* **Goal**: Establish the pain point.
* **Pitch**: *"Currently, your operations are scattered across Excel sheets, WhatsApp groups, and billing tools. TravelOrgOS gives you one unified operating system from trip configuration to traveller payment and fleet scheduling."*

---

## 2. Walkthrough Steps

### Step 1: Admin Dashboard (2 Minutes)
- **Action**: Log in as `owner@demo-travel.com`. Point out the live KPIs tracking active trips, total bookings, revenue collected, and pending balances.
- **Pitch**: *"A single screen tells you what requires attention today, how much cash flow is outstanding, and which trips are approaching capacity."*

### Step 2: 11-Step Trip Builder & Certified Resource Assignment (3 Minutes)
- **Action**: Navigate to **Trip Management** ➔ Click **Create Trip**.
- **Action**: Step through the builder. Navigate to **Step 6: Assign Tour Guides** and assign a guide (e.g. Sarah Jenkins). Save the trip.
- **Action**: Create another trip overlapping the dates of the first trip. Re-assign the same guide.
- **Visual Alert**: Show the validation warning blocking the assignment: *"GUIDE CONFLICT: Guide Sarah Jenkins is already assigned to another trip during this timeframe!"*
- **Pitch**: *"Our conflict engine acts as a safety barrier, preventing operators from double-booking tour guides, SUV fleets, or hotel rooms."*

### Step 3: Branded Mobile Portal & Indian GST splits (3 Minutes)
- **Action**: Navigate to `/portal/demo-travel`. Open the trip card details.
- **Action**: Select 2 seats, enter traveller details, select billing state (e.g. Karnataka vs. Tamil Nadu). Renders the exact CGST/SGST/IGST tax splits.
- **Action**: Click Book and mock checkout.
- **Pitch**: *"Your travellers get a branded booking portal. The system calculates local taxes automatically, deduplicates CRM records, and locks seats safely."*

### Step 4: Ledger & Balance Verification (2 Minutes)
- **Action**: Return to the admin dashboard. Show that seat counts, outstanding balance, and ledger metrics update instantly.
- **Pitch**: *"Every transaction updates your accounting balance and operations dashboard instantly. No manually reconciling spreadsheets required."*
